About this data

This prototype uses the available January 2026 DWLR dataset to display recorded Depth to Water Level (DTWL) observations.

These records do not establish real-time groundwater availability. A nearby observation may not represent groundwater conditions at your exact location. Green, yellow, and red availability indicators will be introduced only after a suitable assessment method has been established.
